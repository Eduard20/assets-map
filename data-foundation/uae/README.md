# UAE source-qualified portal subset

Tracking: https://github.com/Eduard20/assets-map/issues/53

This directory contains minimal factual input to the existing foundation validator, not copies of publisher reports. The portal projects only the exact reviewed candidate set. Selected official WAM news statements and an SEC-hosted ownership-chain disclosure replace previously held publisher-report candidates. Previously staged e& annual-report, du and Al Masraf records are not admitted through this bundle.

The UAE view aggregates independently reviewed, hash-pinned releases into one searchable inventory. Each research release remains capped at 30 claims; the combined inventory is not truncated to that limit. The view preserves dated group membership separately from direct equity ownership and non-equity asset/brand affiliations. Source-named business groups stay typed as reported groups where legal-entity resolution is not established. An unspecified equity interest never invents an ordinary share class, listing, security identifier or share quantity. Reported group interests do not imply immediate legal title, voting rights or control.

Source review is specific to selected non-expressive facts, attribution and channels. It does not claim WAM articles are openly licensed, grant general collection permission, or authorize copying prose, images, branding, reports or proprietary identifiers. Restricted ADQ/Mubadala/Dubai Holding publisher channels and unreviewed broader holdings remain outside the release.

Publication date, reporting date and observation date remain distinct. A null publication date is shown as unknown. An observed-by date does not refresh an old ownership snapshot. The 2026 Mubadala activity announcement discusses 2025 activity; it does not date every acquisition to publication day.

The exact bundle, independent acceptance and maintainer decision are pinned by SHA256 manifest. Public export is reproducible via `node scripts/export-uae.mjs --check`. Every changed input requires re-review and a new matching bundle manifest; the app never consumes an unreviewed research inventory.

Coverage remains deliberately partial across federal investors, Abu Dhabi, Dubai, northern emirates and listed/private groups. Included groups have selected disclosures, not complete portfolios. Full subsidiaries, privately held positions, undisclosed stakes and subsequent ownership changes remain major gaps. Do not describe the result as all UAE holdings or sum group and subsidiary interests.

See docs/uae-admission.md for schema and publication boundaries.
